# IMPORTANT — your current D8/D9 wiring on ATmega2560

You previously specified Nextion RX/TX on Mega2560 digital pins 8 and 9.

With the standard Arduino `SoftwareSerial` library, Mega/Mega2560 cannot reliably use D8/D9 as a
software-serial receive pair. The standard receive-capable pins are a limited set (for example
10–15, 50–53, and A8–A15).

For this project, the recommended connection is the Mega2560 hardware UART:
- Nextion TX -> Mega RX1 (pin 19)
- Nextion RX -> Mega TX1 (pin 18)
- GND -> GND
- `Serial1.begin(115200);`

If the PCB is already fixed to D8/D9, do not blindly copy the Serial1 example. Keep your existing
physical wiring only if the serial library you use explicitly supports RX on D8 for Mega2560 and
you have verified bidirectional communication. Otherwise, move the link to Serial1.
